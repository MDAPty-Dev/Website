Refund and cancellation policy

KhumoStax · Version 2026-11c · Matshele Data Advisory (Pty) Ltd

This policy applies to KhumoStax subscriptions bought from Matshele Data Advisory (Pty) Ltd.

1. Cancelling

  • Cancel at any time from Plan & Billing in the app, or by emailing reamogetswe@matsheledata.co.za. No cancellation fee.
  • Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for; you are not billed again.
  • You can still sign in and export your data after cancelling, as described in the Terms of Service.

2. Refunds

  • First payment: if you change your mind, ask within 7 days of your first payment and we refund it in full.
  • Yearly plans: ask within 14 days of the payment for a full refund. After that, the plan runs to the end of the year and is not refunded in part.
  • Monthly plans: after the first 7 days, the current month is not refunded in part.
  • Charged twice, or charged after you cancelled: we refund the extra amount in full.
  • Where the law gives you more rights, for example a cooling-off period under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, those rights apply.

3. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through PayFast (or by EFT if you paid by EFT), within 7 business days of our approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

4. Changing plans

Upgrading takes effect immediately and the new price applies from your next invoice. Moving to a cheaper plan or to Freemium also takes effect immediately; your data is kept, and what you have already paid for the current period is not refunded in part, except as set out above. Switching between monthly and yearly billing takes effect at your next renewal.

5. Contact

Refund requests and questions: reamogetswe@matsheledata.co.za.
