Data Processing Agreement

KhumoStax · Version 2026-11d · Matshele Data Advisory (Pty) Ltd

This agreement is part of the KhumoStax Terms of Service. It is between the business that registers a KhumoStax account ("the Customer") and Matshele Data Advisory (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2025/498348/07 ("we"). It is the written contract that sections 20 and 21 of POPIA require between a responsible party and its operator. Words such as personal information, processing, data subject, responsible party and operator have the meanings POPIA gives them.

1. Roles

1.1 The Customer is the responsible party for personal information it, or its users, put into KhumoStax about its employees, clients, suppliers and other people ("Customer Data").

1.2 We are the Customer's operator for Customer Data. Annex 1 describes the processing.

2. Our instructions

2.1 We process Customer Data only to provide KhumoStax under the Terms, and as the Customer directs through its settings and use of the features. These are the Customer's documented instructions.

2.2 We do not use Customer Data for our own purposes, sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models.

2.3 If the law requires us to process Customer Data in another way, we will tell the Customer first unless the law forbids it.

3. The Customer's responsibilities

3.1 The Customer has a lawful basis under POPIA for the personal information it puts into KhumoStax, and tells its employees, clients and suppliers how it uses their information.

3.2 The Customer decides who its users are and what role each has, and keeps passwords private.

3.3 The Customer keeps the information accurate and up to date.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 We treat Customer Data as confidential and do not disclose it except as this agreement allows.

4.2 Only our people who need it to run or support the service can reach Customer Data. They are bound to confidentiality. They look at a Customer's records only to give support the Customer asked for, to fix a fault, or where the law requires it.

5. Security

We keep the security measures in Annex 3 in place, as section 19 of POPIA requires, and improve them over time. We will not reduce the overall level of protection.

6. Sub-operators

6.1 The Customer agrees that we use the sub-operators in Annex 2. Each is bound by a written agreement that protects Customer Data at least as well as this one.

6.2 We give the Customer at least 30 days' notice by email before adding or replacing a sub-operator. If the Customer objects on reasonable grounds, it may cancel its subscription and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

7. Transfers outside South Africa

Customer Data is stored in South Africa. Receipt images go to Amazon Textract in Ireland only if the Customer switches on automatic receipt reading. Ireland is subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation, which meets the test in section 72 of POPIA. We make no other transfer without updating Annex 2 under clause 6.2.

8. Security compromises

8.1 If we have reasonable grounds to believe Customer Data has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we notify the Customer immediately, and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware, as section 21(2) requires.

8.2 The notice says what happened, what information and how many people are likely affected, what we have done, and who to contact.

8.3 We help the Customer notify the Information Regulator and the people affected under section 22.

9. Requests from people

If an employee, client or supplier of the Customer asks us directly to see, correct or delete their information, we pass the request to the Customer within five working days and do not answer it ourselves unless the Customer asks us to. We help the Customer respond, using the export and correction tools in KhumoStax.

10. Returning and deleting Customer Data

10.1 The Customer can download its data at any time while its account is active or read-only.

10.2 When the Customer asks for deletion, or 90 days after the account closes, we delete Customer Data as set out in the Privacy Notice. Backups that still hold it expire within 35 days.

10.3 We keep only what the law requires us to keep.

11. Showing compliance

On written request, once a year, we give the Customer the information reasonably needed to show that we meet this agreement, including a summary of our security measures and sub-operators.

12. Duration and liability

This agreement lasts for as long as we hold Customer Data. Liability under it is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service, except where the law does not allow a limit.

Annex 1. Details of the processing

People concerned The Customer's employees, contractors, clients, suppliers, and their contact people
Kinds of information Names, contact details, ID or passport numbers, tax numbers, bank details, salaries, deductions, leave, claims, trips, invoices, payments, receipts, uploaded documents
Special personal information Not required by KhumoStax. The Customer should not upload it (for example health details in a leave note) unless it has a lawful basis
Purposes Invoicing, expenses, bank statement matching, payroll and payslips, leave and claims, procurement, compliance documents, reporting and the accountant export
Duration The life of the Customer's account, then deletion under clause 10

Annex 2. Sub-operators

Sub-operator What it does Where
Amazon Web Services Hosting, database, file storage, backups, email (SES) Africa (Cape Town) region
Amazon Web Services (Textract) Reading receipt images, only if the Customer switches it on Europe (Ireland) region

Paystack handles the Customer's subscription payments to us. It processes our billing information, not Customer Data.

Annex 3. Security measures

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (database, files and backups).
  • Separation between customers enforced by the application and by database row-level security.
  • Role-based access within each Customer, with employees limited to their own records.
  • Automatic sign-out after inactivity (the Customer sets the limits) and a 12-hour maximum session.
  • Bank statement files and passwords are never stored; changes to imported statements need a second person's approval.
  • An audit trail of changes, approvals and data exports.
  • Daily backups, copied to a separate, locked account, and a written recovery plan.
  • Automated security tests on every release.
