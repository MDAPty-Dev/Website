About us

Bank-grade data discipline, made available to everyone

Matshele Data Advisory was founded by Reamogetswe Matshele, a data architect with more than ten years in South African financial services, across credit risk, regulatory reporting, data governance and cloud data platforms.

We started with a simple idea: the controls that keep large institutions' data reliable should not be out of reach for a growing business. We bring that discipline to advisory clients, and we built it into KhumoStax.
