2. This website

This website has no forms. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. If you email us, we use your details only to reply to you and keep the email for as long as needed for that conversation.

Google Analytics, only if you agree. When you first visit, we ask whether we may use Google Analytics cookies to see how visitors use the site: which pages are read, how long for, roughly which country and what kind of device. Nothing is loaded and no cookie is set unless you click Accept. We do not use this for advertising, we have switched off Google's advertising features, and Google Analytics does not store your IP address. Google processes this information for us in the United States and elsewhere under its data processing terms, which provide the protection section 72 of POPIA requires. The cookies (named _ga) last up to two years, and the reports are kept for 14 months. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

3. KhumoStax: our two roles

  • For your own account (your name, email, billing contact and sign-in activity), we decide how the information is used. POPIA calls us the responsible party.
  • For what your business puts into KhumoStax about its employees, clients and suppliers, your business is the responsible party. We are its operator: we process that information only on your business's instructions and only to run the service. Our Data Processing Agreement sets out these terms.

4. What we collect

  • Account and contact details of users.
  • Business records you enter: quotes, invoices, expenses, bills, purchase orders, leave, claims and trips.
  • Employee details your business enters for payroll, such as ID or passport numbers, tax numbers, bank details and salaries.
  • Bank statement lines you import. The statement file and any PDF password are used once to read the transactions and are never stored.
  • Receipt photos and supplier invoice PDFs you upload.
  • Payment records for your subscription. Card, Instant EFT and Capitec Pay payments happen on Paystack's secure pages. We never see or store card numbers; if you ask us to keep your card, we hold only a token that lets us charge your renewals.
  • Security records: sign-in times, sessions and an audit trail of changes and exports.
  • Usage counts: how many times each part of KhumoStax is opened per company per day. Nothing about what was looked at or typed.

6. Where it is stored, and transfers outside South Africa

KhumoStax runs on Amazon Web Services in the Africa (Cape Town) region, and email is sent through Amazon SES in the same region. One feature sends data outside South Africa: if your business switches on automatic receipt reading, the receipt image is sent to Amazon Textract in Ireland to read the supplier, date and amounts. Ireland is bound by the EU General Data Protection Regulation, which gives protection at least as strong as POPIA, as section 72 requires. We have told AWS not to use these images to improve its own services. Businesses that do not want this can type receipts in by hand.

7. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help run KhumoStax, under agreements that require them to protect it: Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage, email and receipt reading), Paystack (payments), and our email provider for our own mailboxes. We share information with authorities only where the law requires it.

8. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Each business's records are kept apart from every other business's, enforced by the application and by the database itself.
  • Roles within each business. Employees see only their own payslips and records.
  • Automatic sign-out after a period of inactivity, shorter for employees.
  • Changes to imported bank statements need a second person's approval, and every change and export is logged.
  • Daily encrypted backups, with a copy in a separate, locked account.

9. If something goes wrong

If we have reasonable grounds to believe that personal information has been accessed or acquired by someone not authorised to, we will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected as soon as reasonably possible, as section 22 requires. Where the information belongs to your business's employees, clients or suppliers, we tell your business straight away and help it notify them.

10. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open.
  • When a business asks for deletion in Settings → Privacy, we email a full copy, delete everything seven days later (the request can be cancelled until then) and email a final copy. Backups that still hold the data expire within 35 days.
  • Our own invoices and payment records for your subscription are kept for five years, as tax law requires.

11. Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. You may also object to its use, and withdraw consent to product news at any time. A business can download its data or ask for deletion itself in Settings → Privacy. If you are an employee, client or supplier of a business that uses KhumoStax, please contact that business first; we will help it respond.

Write to our Information Officer at reamogetswe@matsheledata.co.za. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg; inforegulator.org.za.
