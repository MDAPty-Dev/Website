Privacy Notice (POPIA)

KhumoStax · Version 2026-11c · Matshele Data Advisory (Pty) Ltd

This notice explains how Matshele Data Advisory (Pty) Ltd, operator of KhumoStax and of this website, handles personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. This website

This website has no forms, cookies or tracking. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. If you email us, we use your details only to reply to you and keep the email for as long as needed for that conversation.

2. KhumoStax: two roles

  • For your own account details (name, email, billing contact, sign-in activity) we are the responsible party.
  • For information your business puts into KhumoStax about employees, clients and suppliers, your business is the responsible party and we are its operator: we process that information only on your instructions and to run the service.

3. What we collect

  • Account and contact details of users.
  • Business records you enter: invoices, expenses, payroll, leave, claims, purchase orders.
  • Bank statement lines you import. The statement file itself and any PDF password are used once to read the transactions and are not stored.
  • Receipt photos you upload.
  • Payment records for your subscription. Card and Instant EFT payments are handled on PayFast’s secure pages (PCI-DSS certified). We never see or store card numbers; if you ask us to keep your card, we hold only a token that lets us charge your renewals.
  • Technical logs needed for security, such as sign-in times and audit trails.

4. Why we use it

To provide and secure the service, bill for it, send the emails you or your administrator switch on (invoices, approvals, billing), give support, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information and we do not use your business data for advertising.

5. Where it is stored

KhumoStax is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the Africa (Cape Town) region. Each business’s data is kept separate from every other business’s. If automatic receipt reading is switched on, the receipt image is sent to Amazon Textract in Ireland (EU) to read it, a country with data protection law comparable to POPIA. Emails are sent through Amazon SES.

6. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help run KhumoStax (AWS for hosting, storage, email and receipt reading; PayFast, and Paystack as a back-up, for payments; our email provider for our own mailboxes), under agreements that require them to protect it, or where the law requires.

7. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Strict separation between businesses, enforced in the database itself.
  • Role-based access within each business, and an audit trail of changes and data extracts.
  • Regular backups.

If we become aware of a security compromise affecting personal information we will notify the affected business and, where required, the Information Regulator.

8. How long we keep it

While your account is active. The account owner can download a copy and ask for deletion at any time; deleted data is removed after a seven-day cooling-off period. Financial records of your subscription are kept for five years where tax law requires.

9. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing. A business using KhumoStax can download a copy of its data and ask for deletion itself in Settings → Privacy. Employees and clients of a KhumoStax customer should contact that business first. Contact our Information Officer (Reamogetswe Matshele) at reamogetswe@matsheledata.co.za. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg.org.za.
