Security and privacy

KhumoStax is built with the security habits of a bank

We spent a decade protecting data in South African banks. Those habits are built into KhumoStax, and they are on for every company, on every plan.

Kept in South Africa, encrypted

Your records are stored in Amazon Web Services’ Cape Town region, encrypted on disk and on every connection.

Walled off from other companies

Two separate locks keep each company apart: the app checks every request, and the database itself refuses to show another company’s rows.

Two-step sign-in

A code from an authenticator app after the password, so a stolen password isn’t enough. Owners can make it compulsory for admins.

Only what each person needs

Staff see only their own payslips, leave and claims, and everyone is signed out after a period of inactivity the owner sets.

Changes need a second person

Payroll, purchase orders and changes to bank lines already in the books need approval. Every sign-in and change is in the audit trail.

Watched around the clock

AWS threat detection watches our systems day and night, every change to them is recorded in a log that can’t be altered, and we’re alerted straight away.

Attacked before every release

Each update faces over a thousand automated attack attempts, such as one company trying to reach another’s records. If one gets through, the update doesn’t ship.

Backed up every day

Daily backups, and the database can be restored to any minute in the last 14 days.

POPIA: our Information Officer is registered with the Information Regulator, and a Data Processing Agreement comes with every account. Owners can download or delete their company’s data at any time. Bank statement files and their passwords are never stored, and card details stay with Paystack: KhumoStax never sees them. Read the Privacy Notice.
